    enum Color

    pub enum Color :: [Equatable, Debug]

    Conforms to Equatable, Debug.

    A terminal color: one of the 16 of ECMA-48 and aixterm, one of the 256, or RGB.

    Out-of-range components of Color.Index and Color.Rgb are clamped to 0..255.
