    struct Date

    pub struct Date :: [Equatable, Ordered, TotalOrder, Hashable, Display, Parse]

    Conforms to Equatable, Ordered, TotalOrder, Hashable, Display, Parse.

    A day of the proleptic Gregorian calendar. Its text is ISO 8601's, 2026-10-08; a year outside 0000–9999 shows its sign and its digits, +10000-01-01, which RFC 3339 does not read back.

    Methods

    pub static fn of(year: int, month: int, day: int): Date throws TimeError

    Returns the date year-month-day.

    Errors

    Throws TimeError where there is no such date: a month outside 1–12, a day beyond the month's.

    Returns whether the date's year has a February 29th: divisible by 4, and by 400 where by 100.

    pub fn plusDays(n: int): Date

    pub fn show(): string
    pub static fn parse(s: StringView): Date throws ParseError

    Parses RFC 3339's full-date: YYYY-MM-DD, four digits of year, a date that exists.

    Errors

    Throws ParseError with kind Empty for an empty text and Invalid for a text of another shape or a date that does not exist.
