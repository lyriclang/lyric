    Conforms to Equatable, Ordered, TotalOrder, Hashable, Default, Display, Parse, Add, Sub, Mul<int>, Div<int>.

    A span of time in whole nanoseconds, negative or not: some 292 years either way.

    Panics

    Arithmetic that leaves that range panics with an overflow (RT0002), as int's does.

    pub fn show(): string

    Returns the span as text: 0s; under a second one unit — ns, µs, ms — with the fraction, its trailing zeros dropped (1.5µs, 500ms); from a second on hours, minutes and seconds with their fraction, the larger ones from the first that is not zero (1.5s, 1m30s, 1h0m0s); a minus in front of a negative one.

    pub static fn parse(s: StringView): Duration throws ParseError

    Parses a span: an optional sign, then one or more numbers each with its unit — ns, us (or µs, μs), ms, s, m, h —, a number with a fraction allowed: 1h30m, 1.5h, -300ms. 0 alone is zero. A fraction is scaled to its unit through a float: the nanoseconds of 1.5h are exact, those of a long fraction of an hour may be rounded.

    Errors

    Throws ParseError with kind Empty for an empty text, Invalid for a number without digits or a missing or unknown unit, and Overflow where the span does not fit.
