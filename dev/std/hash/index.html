    std.hash

    Provides the hashers and the checksums of common formats and protocols.

    DefaultHasher is SipHash-1-3 under a key the system draws once per process: a Map hashes with it, its order differs from run to run, and no input from outside can aim collisions at it. FixedHasher is the same under a zero key, for what must hash alike in every run; Fnv1a64 is fast and keyless, for trusted input. All three implement Hasher. The checksums are crc32, crc32c and adler32.

    Types

    DefaultHasher struct — Hashes with SipHash-1-3 under the process's key, drawn from the system at the first use.
    FixedHasher struct — Hashes with SipHash-1-3 under a zero key.
    Fnv1a64 struct — Hashes with 64-bit FNV-1a, a byte at a time and without a key.

    Functions

    pub fn crc32(bytes: Slice<uint8>, running: uint32 = 0): uint32

    Returns the CRC-32 (ISO-HDLC, as in zip, gzip and PNG) of bytes, continued from running.

    running is the checksum of what came before, 0 at the start, so the checksum of the whole is that of its pieces, one after the other. crc32("123456789".toBytes()) is 0xCBF43926.

    pub fn crc32c(bytes: Slice<uint8>, running: uint32 = 0): uint32

    Returns the CRC-32C (the Castagnoli polynomial, as in iSCSI, RFC 3720) of bytes, continued from running.

    running is the checksum of what came before, 0 at the start. crc32c("123456789".toBytes()) is 0xE3069283.

    pub fn adler32(bytes: Slice<uint8>, running: uint32 = 1): uint32

    Returns the Adler-32 (RFC 1950) of bytes, continued from running.

    running is the checksum of what came before, 1 at the start. adler32("Wikipedia".toBytes()) is 0x11E60398.
