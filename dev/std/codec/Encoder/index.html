    interface Encoder

    pub interface Encoder

    The writing side of a format: the scalars, and the containers as begin/end around their parts. The sequence of calls a conformance makes is the contract: beginSeq(n), then n values, then endSeq(); beginMap(n), then n times beginKey(), a key and a value, then endMap(); beginStruct(name, n), then n times beginField(name) and a value, then endStruct(); beginVariant(…, hasPayload), then the payload where there is one, then endVariant(). A format that cannot hold a form throws EncodeError, with path() as its place.
